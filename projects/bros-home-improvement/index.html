The Bros Improvement is a full-service home improvement and general contracting company based in Danbury, Connecticut. We specialize in managing every step of residential projects, from framing and trim carpentry to full home additions and deck renovations.

Serving Danbury, New Milford, Redding, Ridgefield, Weston, Wilton, Norwalk, New Fairfield, Newtown, Westport, New Canaan, and Greenwich, we are committed to quality, craftsmanship, and timely project delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1. Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes! The Bros Improvement is fully licensed and insured, so your home and investment are always protected.

  • Q2. What areas do you serve?

    We serve Danbury, New Milford, Redding, Ridgefield, Weston, Wilton, Norwalk, New Fairfield, Newtown, Westport, New Canaan, and Greenwich.

  • Q3. Do you provide free estimates?

    Yes, every project starts with a free, no-obligation consultation and estimate.

  • Q4. How long does a typical project take?

    Timelines depend on scope — a trim carpentry job may take days, while a full home addition takes several weeks. We give you a clear schedule before work begins.

  • Q5. What types of projects do you handle?

    General contracting, framing, trim carpentry, deck building, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and full home additions.

  • Q6. Do you handle bathroom remodeling?

    Yes! We offer full bathroom remodeling services, including design, layout, fixtures, and finishes, tailored to your style and needs.

  • Q7. Can you help with home additions?

    Absolutely. We manage full home additions start to finish, including framing and trim work.

  • Q8. Do you offer deck construction and renovation?

    Yes, custom deck construction and renovation for outdoor living spaces is one of our core services.

  • Q9. How do I schedule a consultation or estimate?

    Fill out the free quote form on this page or call us at (203) 682-5912 and we'll set up a time that works for you.

  • Q10. Do you work on both small and large projects?

    Yes — from a single trim carpentry fix to a full home addition, we manage projects of every size.
