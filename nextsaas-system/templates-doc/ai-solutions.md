# AI Solutions Template (NextSaaS)

Complete SaaS landing page template optimized for AI solutions and technology products. **5,569 lines, ~47 distinct sections, responsive across mobile/tablet/desktop.**

## Páginas disponíveis

- **index.html** — Main landing page (hero to footer)
- about.html — Company information
- services.html — Service offerings
- pricing.html — Pricing page
- projects.html — Portfolio/case studies
- project-details.html — Individual project showcase
- blog.html — Blog listing
- blog-details.html — Blog article
- team-details.html — Team member profiles
- contact.html — Contact form
- faq.html — FAQ section
- signup.html — Registration
- login.html — Authentication
- service-details.html — Service deep-dive
- 404.html — Error page

## Seções (index.html)

1. **Header + Navigation** — Fixed navbar with mega-menus, mobile hamburger, "Try now" CTA
2. **Hero Section** — Split text animation, video CTA buttons, hero image with gradient overlay
3. **Our Impact (Stats)** — Counter cards (500+ projects, 98% satisfaction), stacked testimonial cards
4. **Empowering Your Business** — 3-column feature cards with glowing borders, workflow automation showcase
5. **Innovative Solutions** — Grid of 4 solution cards (Intelligence Automation, Predictive Analytics, AI Chatbot, Data Analytics)
6. **Portfolio** — Full-width image cards with hover zoom & overlay
7. **Testimonials** — Rotating cards (5 clients) with 5-star ratings, profile images, roles
8. **FAQ** — Accordion-style collapsible Q&A
9. **Newsletter Signup** — Email subscription form
10. **CTA Section** — "Start AI Transformation" call-to-action
11. **Contact/Connect** — Final engagement section
12. **Footer** — Brand logo, navigation links, branding footer

## Recursos visuais

- **Animations:** `data-opai-animate` (stagger/delay), split-text effects, scroll reveals
- **Gradients:** Radial & linear overlays; blend modes (mix-blend-plus-lighter); colored glows
- **Icons:** Custom SVG library (next-sass font); stripe-based iconography
- **Images:** 100+ JPG/PNG assets (hero, projects, testimonials, placeholders)
- **Video:** Hero background video (hero-bg.mp4) + YouTube embeds
- **Components:** Modal video player, dropdown menus, counter animations, testimonial carousels
- **Accessibility:** ARIA labels, semantic HTML (section > h2), alt text on images

## Notas de qualidade

**Strengths:**
- Highly polished, production-ready design
- Excellent mobile responsiveness (Tailwind CSS, 12-column grid)
- Rich microinteractions (hover states, stagger animations)
- Semantic markup + accessibility attributes
- Professional typography hierarchy (Sora/Inter Tight combo)

**Observations:**
- Heavy JavaScript dependency for animations (animations may lag on low-end devices)
- Color scheme deeply locked to dark (#0d1017) + light contrast (#f8f9fa)
- Limited reusability of card patterns (custom class combinations)
- Large CSS bundle (main.css comprehensive, not minified in template)
- Image assets are placeholders — need brand-specific replacements

**Technical Stack:**
- **CSS:** Tailwind CSS (custom theme), custom utility classes
- **JS:** Vanilla JS (data attributes drive behavior)
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts (Sora, Inter Tight, IBM Plex Mono, Manrope, Space Grotesk)
- **Structure:** Semantic HTML5, mobile-first approach
- **Build:** Pre-built HTML (no build step; open & use)
